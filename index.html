FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our security services across Nigeria.

How quickly can you deploy security officers?

We offer rapid deployment across Nigeria within 2 to 4 hours for urgent requests. For planned events or long-term contracts, we recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance to ensure optimal team selection and briefing.

Are your security officers licensed and vetted?

Absolutely. Every officer undergoes rigorous background checks, psychological evaluations, and continuous training. All armed personnel hold valid licenses issued by the Nigerian Police Force and comply with NSCDC regulations.

Do you provide security for events and private parties?

Yes, we specialize in event security including weddings, concerts, corporate gatherings, and private parties. Our team handles access control, crowd management, VIP escort, and emergency response protocols.

What areas in Nigeria do you cover?

We operate across all major cities in Nigeria including Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano, Ibadan, and beyond. Our mobile units can reach any location within Nigeria's major urban centers.

Can I hire a bodyguard for international travel?

Yes, our executive protection team includes travel security specialists who can accompany clients on domestic and international trips. We handle logistics, route planning, and coordination with local security at destinations.
